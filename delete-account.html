SubTracker Hub - Account & Data Deletion

Developer: KTX Technology

Application: SubTracker Hub

How to Request Account Deletion

Users can delete their account and associated data directly within the app:

  1. Open the SubTracker Hub app.
  2. Navigate to Settings / Profile.
  3. Tap on Delete Account and confirm your request.

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing us at: kayafrat99@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My SubTracker Account".

What Data is Deleted?

Upon account deletion, all personal data, subscription records, and account credentials stored in Firebase will be permanently and immediately removed from our servers.

Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data after your account deletion request is processed.
