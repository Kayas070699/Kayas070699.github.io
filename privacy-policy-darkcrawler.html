Privacy Policy for Dark Crawler

Last updated: November 2026

KTX Technology operates the mobile game Dark Crawler. This page explains what information the game and the services it uses handle when you play.

1. Information we collect

Dark Crawler does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contact list. Your game progress, high scores, settings and in-game credits are stored on your own device. We do not run servers that hold player profiles.

2. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads (rewarded videos and full-screen ads between rounds). Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners, including Unity Ads and InMobi. To show ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and run analytics, these services may collect and use:

  • your device advertising ID (Google Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • your precise location, but only if you have granted the game the location permission (see section 3);
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, language) and diagnostic or crash information;
  • how you interact with ads.

This information may be shared with the ad partners. Their own policies apply, for example Unity and InMobi.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

3. Arena (local multiplayer) and location permission

Arena duels connect two nearby devices directly over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi. Match data goes only between the two devices; we do not receive it. On Android 12 and older, Android requires the location permission to find nearby devices, so the game asks for it when you start a Bluetooth duel. The game uses it only to find the other player and does not send your location to us. Granting it is optional: you can deny it, or turn it off later in your Android settings, and still play the rest of the game. While the permission is granted, the ad services listed in section 2 may also read your device's precise location.

On Android 13 and newer, Arena uses the "Nearby devices" permission instead, which does not give access to your location.

4. In-app purchases

Optional credit packs are bought through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google; we do not receive or store your payment card details.

5. iPhone and iPad version

The iOS version of Dark Crawler (App Store) contains no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no Arena multiplayer. It does not collect any data: progress is stored only on your device, and the game does not ask for tracking, location or Bluetooth access. Sections 2, 3 and 4 apply to the Android version only.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data about you on our own servers. Uninstalling the game removes the progress stored on your device. Data held by our ad partners is kept under their policies; to ask for access or deletion you can write to us and we will pass the request on, or contact the partner directly.

7. Children's privacy

Dark Crawler is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
