Privacy Policy for Dark Crawler

Last updated: October 2026

KTX Technology operates the mobile game Dark Crawler. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you play our game.

1. Information Collection and Use

Dark Crawler is designed as a standalone offline arcade game. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, device ID, or location).

2. Data Retention and Storage

All in-game progress, high scores, and credits are stored strictly locally on your own device. We do not maintain external database servers for player profiles.

3. Third-Party Services

The game currently does not integrate third-party tracking or advertising SDKs collecting personal user data.

4. Children's Privacy

Our game is safe for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.
